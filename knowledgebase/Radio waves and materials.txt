Project name: Radio waves and materials

How do radio waves behave?
Which materials influence them? Radio waves enable a wide range of wireless digital 
applications. These include smartphones, smart homes, and autonomous vehicles. 
Radio waves always emit from an antenna. Antennas can emit in different ways: for example, 
directionally, that is, in a specific direction, or spherically in all directions. 
Different materials affect radio waves. Some allow radio waves to pass through, while others 
weaken or block them completely. Certain surfaces can also reflect radio waves, similar to how 
a mirror reflects light. Therefore, an understanding of environmental factors is important for 
research into radio communication.

What problem do radio waves solve?
Radio waves enable wireless communication between technical devices. They make these 
devices mobile and flexible in use. They are the basis of modern communication and connect 
people worldwide.

What you can discover here:
 Move the transmitter antenna while holding down the activation button. This will allow you to 
see how radio waves spread through the air. Observe how the receiver antennas change color 
depending on the radio waves they receive. 
Experiment with different materials between the transmitter and receiver antennas. This will 
allow you to see how materials influence the path of radio waves. 
Pick up a reflective surface. This will help you understand how radio waves can overcome an 
obstacle on their way to the receiver antenna by finding new paths  via detours.
