Project name: Artificial movements – The body language of robots

Is there such a thing as robot gestures? 
Robots move – sometimes purely functionally, sometimes in ways that resemble human 
movements. But how are these movements created, and how do we perceive them? 

The students’ task at the Faculty of Design of Dresden University of Applied Sciences: 
Students develop new forms of robotic gestures. They design movement patterns that are not 
directly copied from humans or animals, and test whether these movements still make sense to 
us. Their work is interdisciplinary: they sketch ideas, build models, program movement 
sequences, and work with 3D technology, mechanics, and sensors. The goal is to find out how 
robots can move in ways that allow us to intuitively understand their gestures – even if they do 
not resemble our own.

What you can discover here: 
Give the robot a task – and the robot will carry it out. Afterwards, you can evaluate how its 
movements felt to you
