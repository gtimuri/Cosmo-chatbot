Project name: Strong materials for soft robotic hands

What problem does this innovation aim to solve?
Metal robot grippers can damage delicate objects when handling them. Soft grippers can adapt 
well to the shape of an object and handle it gently. However, they are not capable of holding 
heavy loads.

What changes does this innovation bring? 
The new robotic hands are made from a combination of soft and rigid materials. For example, 
they use joints made of silicone and fiber-reinforced plastic. These can deform in a controlled 
way while exerting greater gripping or hol ding force – allowing even heavy objects to be handled 
safely.

Who will benefit from this innovation? 
All people living in a digitalized society.

What is the technology behind it and how does it work? 
The soft plastics are embedded together with rigid structures into a textile base material. This 
creates a gripper that is both flexible and strong at the same time.

Challenges 
• The new soft robot hands are to be manufactured industrially. 
• New rules are needed to ensure that these robot hands can be used safely in the future.
 
When will this innovation be available?
Approximately 2030 to 2035
