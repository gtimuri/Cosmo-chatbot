Project name: Light-based communication for field robots

What problem does this innovation aim to solve?
More and more robots are assisting with agricultural work. They autonomously navigate across 
fields and will occasionally encounter people out on a walk or farm workers. Those persons 
need to be able to interpret the field robot’s behaviour in order to feel safe. Clear light signals 
can help with this.

What changes does this innovation bring? 
Field robots should be able to clearly show what they are currently doing and what they plan to 
do next. This allows people to “understand” them and build trust. Researchers are developing a 
new light-based signaling system for this purpose. The field robots are equipped with highly 
visible LEDs that indicate, for example, whether the robot is moving, stopping, or about to turn. 
This helps prevent accidents, and strengthens trust in the technology.

Who will benefit from this innovation? 
• Experts who work with robots in the field. 
• People out on a walk near fields.
 • Companies that build agricultural field robots and may later develop other types of robots as 
well.

What is the technology behind it and how does it work?  
The researchers are developing a new lighting system made up of many small LED lights. Each 
LED can be controlled individually. The LEDs can shine in differ ent colors, brightness levels, or 
patterns of movement. They are arranged in light strips mounted on the field robot. This allows 
the robot to use light to indicate what it is currently doing or what it is about to do.

Challenges 
• The light signals must be clear and easy to understand so that everyone interprets them the 
same way. 
• The meaning of the light signals needs to be consistent everywhere. This requires a 
standardized system. 
• People need to be able to trust autonomous field robots.

When will this innovation be available?
Approximately 2030 to 2040

Who is researching this?
The Chair of Technical Design (TD) at TU Dresden.

What do we see?
A model of a field robot equipped with LEDs designed to communicate with people.
