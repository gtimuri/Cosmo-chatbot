Project name: Energy consumption of mobile communications

What exactly is the problem? 
People are using more and more mobile applications - for example for online shopping, music 
streaming or video conferencing. But it is not just smart phones that send and receive data. 
Cars, sensors and many other devices also access the mobile internet. As a result, the amount 
of data is increasing rapidly. 
Cell towers, routers and other devices transmit these data. They need energy - both during 
production and operation. The more data flows, the higher the energy consumption. 

What is the task of research here? 
Mobile Internet should be possible without placing a greater burden on the environment.
Each new generation of mobile communications can send more data and therefore requires 
more energy. Re searchers are looking for ways to significantly reduce the power consumption 
of 6G. Their aim is to transmit more data but consume less energy.
